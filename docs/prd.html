CONFIDENTIAL & PROPRIETARY — SRA MEDIA INTEGRITY NETWORK

1. Executive Summary & Vision

The SRA TruthGuard platform is an advanced media verification ecosystem designed to combat the exponential surge of digital disinformation, manipulated circulars, coordinated fake news campaigns, and multimodal deepfakes circulating across public discourse and social messaging platforms.

By combining Google AI Studio (Gemini) Intelligence with authoritative ground-truth repositories (Press Information Bureau (PIB), Reserve Bank of India (RBI), Government Gazettes, and global news wire feeds), SRA TruthGuard automates the end-to-end fact-checking pipeline. It provides instant veracity classification (🟢 True, 🔴 False, 🟡 Misleading), confidence scoring (0–100%), sensationalism indexing, and forensic step auditing for journalists, editors, analysts, and citizens.

Core Mission: Democratize high-precision fact-checking through institutional-grade AI, empowering newsrooms and citizens with real-time veracity forensics and transparent verification logs.

4. Functional Requirements & System Modules

4.1. Multimodal Verification Intake Engine

The platform supports 4 distinct verification input modalities accessible via the DeepCheck AI Verifier:

  • Viral Text / Headline Mode: Accepts textual claims, social media forwards, speech quotes, and headline transcripts.
  • Deepfake Video & Media Mode: Analyzes video descriptions, audio transcripts, and spatial-temporal artifact patterns.
  • Official Circular / PDF Mode: Evaluates recruitment notices, ministerial orders, tax policy announcements, and gazette scans.
  • Web Article Link Mode: Ingests external URLs for cross-referencing against verified press bureau feeds.

4.2. Google AI Studio (Gemini) Intelligence Core

The AI layer is powered by Google AI Studio Gemini API with an automated model resilience hierarchy:

Priority Model Candidate Primary Function Fallback Behavior
1 (Primary) gemini-3-flash-preview Ultra-fast multi-channel stance detection & JSON reasoning Fails over to Priority 2 on timeout
2 (Secondary) gemini-3.5-flash High-throughput veracity evaluation & explanation synthesis Fails over to Priority 3 on 503 spike
3 (Tertiary) gemini-3.1-flash-lite Low-latency entity extraction & contextual grounding Fails over to Local Heuristics
4 (Local Fallback) SRA Rule-Based Heuristics Instant deterministic matching against ground-truth registry Guarantees 100% uptime

4.5. Real-Time Forensic Pipeline Logs

Every verification emits structured, sequential step logs displayed live to the user:

  1. [Intake Pipeline]: Ingestion of claim via selected multimodal channel.
  2. [Entity Extraction]: Identification of governmental entities, policy names, and monetary values.
  3. [Forensic Cross-Reference]: Querying PIB, RBI, MoRTH, and press bureau databases.
  4. [Sensationalism Indexing]: Analysis of urgency markers, emotional manipulation, and clickbait density.
  5. [Final Classification]: Verdict allocation with confidence percentage and verifying authority citation.
